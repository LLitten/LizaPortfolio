About

I'm Liza “Zai” Litten, a 2D artist and UI designer from West Virginia, currently pursuing my MS in Game Design and Development at Rochester Institute of Technology.

My interest in video games started in the mid-2010s watching gaming YouTubers talk about the communities and connections they built around games. It's why I focus on creating games that feel meaningful and bring a little light to the people playing them.

My work centers on 2D prop art and UI art/design. I prioritize visual communication by using color and shape language to create intuitive interfaces without cluttering the space with text. Beyond art, I often step into hybrid production roles to help my team manage scope and workflow.
